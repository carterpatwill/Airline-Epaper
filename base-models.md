# Airplane Base Models & Variants (Expanded)

Each **base model** is a distinct silhouette category. The aircraft listed under it share that basic shape, so any airline (or operator) that flies one of those aircraft can have its logo stacked on that base. This expanded set uses finer visual distinctions — 14 base models covering commercial, private, cargo, and a couple of specialty shapes.

## 1. Classic narrow-body (single-aisle)

The everyday single-aisle jet — the most common shape in the sky.

- Boeing 737 (737-700/800/900, 737 MAX)
- Boeing 757
- Airbus A320 family (A319, A320, A321, A320neo, A321neo)
- Boeing 717

## 2. Small narrow-body / large regional

Single-aisle but noticeably slimmer and shorter than a 737/A320.

- Airbus A220 (formerly Bombardier C Series)
- Embraer E-Jet E2 (E190-E2, E195-E2)
- Boeing 737-600 / older short variants

## 3. Regional jet

Small twin-engine jets for short, low-demand routes — often flown by regional partners.

- Embraer E-Jets (E170, E175, E190, E195)
- Bombardier CRJ series (CRJ200, CRJ700, CRJ900)
- Embraer ERJ (ERJ135/140/145)
- Sukhoi Superjet 100

## 4. Turboprop

Propeller-driven regional aircraft — visible props, shorter routes, smaller airports.

- ATR 42 / ATR 72
- De Havilland Dash 8 / Q400 (Bombardier)
- Saab 340 / 2000
- Beechcraft 1900
- Fokker 50

## 5. Twin-aisle wide-body

Two aisles, wider fuselage, twin engines, long-haul range. The flagship of most international carriers.

- Boeing 777 (777-200/300, 777X)
- Boeing 787 Dreamliner (787-8/9/10)
- Boeing 767
- Airbus A330 (A330-200/300, A330neo)
- Airbus A350 (A350-900/1000)

## 6. Four-engine wide-body

Wide-body with four underwing engines (not a jumbo hump).

- Airbus A340 (A340-300/500/600)
- Boeing 747SP (shortened — edge case)
- Ilyushin Il-96

## 7. Tri-jet

Three engines — one buried in the tail. A unmistakable, mostly retired silhouette.

- Boeing 727
- McDonnell Douglas DC-10
- McDonnell Douglas MD-11
- Lockheed L-1011 TriStar
- Boeing 727 / Tu-154

## 8. Jumbo (hump)

Massive fuselage with a partial upper deck creating the signature front hump.

- Boeing 747 (747-100 through 747-8)

## 9. Double-deck

Full-length second deck — the largest passenger jet ever built.

- Airbus A380

## 10. Business jet

Small private/corporate jets with a sleek, tapered silhouette.

- Gulfstream (G550, G650, G700)
- Bombardier (Learjet, Challenger, Global)
- Cessna Citation family
- Dassault Falcon
- Embraer Phenom / Legacy / Praetor

## 11. Piston / light aircraft

Small single- or twin-engine planes — general aviation, flight schools, private
owners. Split by *silhouette shape*, not by model trim: a Cessna 172S, 172N, and
172R are one outline, so there's one bitmap per airframe shape, not per version.
The meaningful GA silhouettes are wing position (high vs low), gear stance
(tricycle vs tailwheel), and engine count.

### 11A. High-wing strut-braced single

Wing on top with visible struts, tricycle gear, one nose prop — the classic
flight-school trainer shape.

- Cessna 172 Skyhawk / 152 / 182 Skylane
- Cessna 206 / 210
- Cessna 208 Caravan (larger, same high-wing family)

### 11B. Low-wing single

Wing at the bottom, clean cantilever (no struts), tricycle gear.

- Cirrus SR20 / SR22
- Piper PA-28 (Cherokee / Archer / Warrior)
- Beechcraft Bonanza
- Diamond DA40

### 11C. Taildragger / classic

High wing sitting nose-up on a tailwheel, slim fabric-style fuselage — vintage,
bush, and aerobatic types.

- American Champion 7ECA Citabria / Decathlon
- Piper J-3 Cub / PA-18 Super Cub
- Aviat Husky

### 11D. Twin piston

Two wing-mounted props — light multi-engine.

- Beechcraft Baron
- Piper Seminole
- Diamond DA42

## 12. Cargo / freighter

Windowless freight aircraft — usually conversions of passenger jets. Home for cargo-carrier logos (FedEx, UPS, DHL, Atlas).

- Boeing 767F / 777F
- Boeing 747 Freighter
- Airbus A330F
- McDonnell Douglas MD-11F
- Boeing 737 / 757 converted freighters

## 13. Seaplane / floatplane

Aircraft with floats or a boat hull for water operations.

- De Havilland Beaver / Otter (on floats)
- Cessna 208 Caravan Amphibian
- ICON A5
- Viking Twin Otter (float variant)

## 14. Prop airliner / vintage

Older piston- and turboprop-powered airliners — a distinct "golden age" silhouette if you want retro liveries.

- Douglas DC-3
- Lockheed Constellation
- Boeing 377 Stratocruiser
- Convair 240/340/440

---

*Notes:*
- *Tri-jets (base 7) and prop airliners (base 14) are mostly retired — include them only if you want vintage/historic liveries.*
- *Freighters (base 12) often share a silhouette with their passenger version; you can keep cargo separate or fold those logos into the matching wide-body/jumbo base.*
- *If you also want military (fighter, bomber, transport, tanker) or helicopters, those would add several more base models on top of these 14.*
